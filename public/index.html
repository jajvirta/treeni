    The time-efficient baseline
    • Frequency is the goal. Aim for 2 sessions/week — 1 is fine on busy weeks. A week with any session keeps your streak.
    • Minimum effective dose. ~4 hard sets per muscle group per week already grows muscle; more has diminishing returns. This isn't about maxing out.
    • Few sets, high effort. 1–2 sets per exercise, taken close to failure (leave 1–2 reps in reserve), is enough.
    • Compound machines. A handful of multi-joint lifts covers the whole body in little time.
    • Supersets save time. Pair pushing + pulling exercises (below) — roughly halves the session with no real loss.
    • Load is flexible. Any weight that brings you near failure in ~6–15 reps builds muscle. Full range of motion; minimal warm-up.
    • Source: Iversen, Norum, Schoenfeld & Fimland (2021), Sports Medicine — "No Time to Lift? Designing Time-Efficient Training Programs for Strength and Hypertrophy."
